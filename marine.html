From simulation to digital twin.

Vessels follow A*-planned routes through a fictional coastal basin. They react to nearby traffic, steer around land and shallow-water exclusion zones, and receive new destinations when they arrive.

This is an exploratory simulator, not a live digital twin. A production marine twin would need actual chart/bathymetry data, AIS or onboard telemetry, current forecasts, calibrated vessel dynamics, and maritime collision regulations.
